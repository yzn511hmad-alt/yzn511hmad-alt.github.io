2. TECH STACK IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core: Vite + TypeScript (Strict Mode) + Semantic HTML5 + Custom CSS (Design Tokens)
- Animation & Motion: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis smooth scroll & velocity tracking
- 3D / WebGL: Three.js + Custom GLSL Shaders (Glacial Mist & Snow Particles)
- Responsive Quality Tiers: DPR capped at min(window.devicePixelRatio, 2.0),
  mobile tiering & IntersectionObserver tab suspension
- Dual-Theme Engine: Dark Mode (Default) & Light Mode with persistent dark surfaces:
  * loader.lua terminal card in Section 02
  * 04 / CHOOSE YOUR ACCESS pricing band
  * READY TO LOAD IN? CTA banner

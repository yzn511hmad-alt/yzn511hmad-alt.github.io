# Aurora footage

**Aurora borealis timelapse**, Harriniva Hotels&Safaris (2013).

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aurora_borealis_timelapse.webm
Original file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/df/Aurora_borealis_timelapse.webm
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The Commons source page identifies the footage as filmed in Swedish and Finnish
Lapland in autumn 2013, with its YouTube CC license reviewed by Commons.

Changes: selected 73–81 seconds, cropped to the upper 1920x650 region, removed
audio, slowed to half speed, joined a reversed copy for a seamless 32-second
loop, encoded H.264. Mobile derivative is 960px wide. The runtime shader keys
out the photographed dark sky/landscape and adds the green aurora emission over
the website sky. No endorsement by the original author is implied.

Files:
- aurora-real-loop.mp4: 1920x650, 32 sec, approximately 3 MB.
- aurora-real-loop-mobile.mp4: 960x326, 32 sec.
- aurora-real-poster.webp: still fallback, extracted at 4 sec.
- PhotographicAurora.ts: same-renderer overlay, no independent animation loop.

Do not claim this is a physical simulation. It is photographed real aurora motion.
The shader aperture is tuned for the current forest plate; resize uses the plate's
crop, not viewport coordinates. A proper sky mask is still recommended if another
plate has branches crossing the central sky region.

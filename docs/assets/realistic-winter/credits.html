Visual credits

Aurora

Aurora artwork supplied for the project by its owner. The supplied original artwork was upscaled locally using official ComfyUI nodes and Real-ESRGAN at 2x resolution, with the original transparency reattached. A local AI-assisted detail reconstruction is blended only inside the upper green arc, preserving the original transparency and unmasked artwork. Its colour and opacity are adjusted during rendering. Curtain motion and diffuse illumination of the snow are animated and composited in real time.

Fire

Photographed flames by Enes Beydilli, under the Pexels license. Cropped and looped.

Environment

Photographic-style landscape artwork generated and composed for Snowy Studios. Interactive ice, walnut box, key and pass use real-time Three.js rendering.

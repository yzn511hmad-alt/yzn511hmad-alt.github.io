Quick answers before you open a ticket.

How long does it take for a support ticket to be answered?

Open a support ticket from the Snowy Discord and our team typically responds within 15–30 minutes during standard operating hours. Weekends and holidays can take a little longer.

I lost access to my premium key, how do I get it back?

Join the Snowy Discord and open a support ticket. Have your Order ID or purchase email ready so the team can verify and restore your access.

How do I report a game patch or a broken script feature?

Open a Discord support ticket with the game, affected feature, and any executor error codes. Screenshots help the team resolve it faster.

Do you offer refunds?

Because Snowy provides digital access keys that are instantly consumed upon use, we do not offer standard refunds. If you have severe technical issues our support team cannot fix within a reasonable timeframe, please reach out to management via Discord.
